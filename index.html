How the Design Process Works

01

Property & Home Information

You'll provide information about your property and home to help establish the septic system requirements and identify any site-specific considerations.

02

Site & Soil Evaluation

The property owner is responsible for arranging and excavating the test pits. You can have your builder dig them or do it yourself if you have suitable equipment available. Using equipment already on-site can help avoid additional excavation costs.

Before digging, request a utility locate through Utility Safety Partners (Alberta One-Call). It's free and helps prevent damage to buried lines.

I'll then evaluate and document the soil conditions, property slope, and required setbacks.

03

System Design & Approval

Based on the soil evaluation results, I'll determine the appropriate septic system type, sizing, and layout. I'll explain the recommended system and the reasons for its selection. The design is then submitted with the permit application for your area.

04

Completed Design

Once the design is approved, I'll provide you with the finalized drawings, supporting documentation, and system maintenance requirements. You can then use these documents to obtain quotes from installers and proceed with installation.

Septic Systems

Finding the right system for your property.

The best system depends on soil conditions, available space, and how the property will be used. Common options include:

Treatment Fields

Underground distribution trenches that use suitable natural soil to treat wastewater.

Treatment Mounds

Raised treatment beds used where natural soil conditions are not suitable for a conventional field.

LFH At-Grade Systems

An option for qualifying forested properties. Treated effluent is spread over the natural forest floor layer (called LFH). Requires secondary treatment.

Open Discharge Systems

An option on qualifying properties where site conditions and applicable requirements allow effluent to discharge onto the ground.

Holding Tanks

Sealed tanks that store wastewater for regular pumping, where permitted.

Septic tanks and additional treatment equipment may also be part of a complete system. I'll explain the recommended design after evaluating your property.
